### 🏥 Building MarkMedi

MarkMedi is a marketing & practice-management platform purpose-built for dermatology and aesthetic clinics. It replaces the usual stack of scheduling app + EHR + CRM + marketing tools with one integrated system.

What I've shipped so far:

- **Patient acquisition** — SEO-optimized clinic pages with online booking, plus an automated digital-positioning diagnosis (geogrid + competitive analysis on Google) used to show clinics exactly where they're losing patients.
- **Scheduling & retention** — automated confirmations and reminders over WhatsApp and email, post-visit NPS capture, and promoter routing to the clinic's Google profile.
- **Cloud EHR** — image-based patient tracking with treatment evolution, body mapping for procedures, and stock deduction on application.
- **CRM & treatment protocols** — patient relationship management and protocol tracking built for the clinic's real workflow.
- **Marketing engine** — drag-and-drop page and email builders, a visual marketing-automation editor (trigger / condition / action), an AI-assisted clinic blog, and teleconsultation with automatic transcription.

The product is in active development and already running in production.
